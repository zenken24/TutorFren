# TutorFren privacy notice (v1, DRAFT for team and teacher review)

**What we keep:** your child's nickname, class and language; their answers, typed or transcribed explanations, and progress; your email for sign-in.
**What we never keep:** voice recordings (voice is turned into text in memory and the recording is deleted straight away), full names, date of birth, address, photos, contacts or location.
**Who sees what:** your child sees their own work. You see a progress summary only (what is mastered, what needs practice, days active this week), never conversations or a timeline. Nobody else's data is ever shown to your child.
**AI:** tutoring replies come from Google Gemini. We send the question, the textbook context and a random learner code; never a name or contact details. Phone numbers and emails typed by a child are removed first.
**Your choices:** you can turn voice input, the progress summary and pilot research on or off at any time, export your child's data, or delete the account. Deleting removes all learning data; copies in backups disappear when backups rotate.
**Keeping it safe:** children have no password; you pair their phone. Guardian mode is locked on a shared phone and sensitive actions need your password again.
**Contact:** Team Future Frame (add contact address before the pilot).
